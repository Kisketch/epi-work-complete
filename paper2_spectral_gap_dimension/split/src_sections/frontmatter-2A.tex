\begin{abstract}
Let $(Q_j)$ be the selected convergent denominators of $e$ preceding the
partial quotients $2j$, and $\Lambda(x)=\limsup j\|Q_jx\|$.
We prove $\Lambda(\mathbf{R})=\frac14\mathbf{Z}_{\ge0}\cup\{\infty\}$,
so every $E_c=\{x:\Lambda\le c\}$ is constant across each gap
$(k/4,(k+1)/4)$ and $\Lambda=0$ exactly on the countable group
$L=\mathbf{Z}+\mathbf{Z}\frac{e+1}{2}$.
Every finite level has Hausdorff dimension $0$, hence
$\{\Lambda<\infty\}$ is null and $\Lambda=\infty$ almost everywhere.
At subcritical weights $\Lambda_\alpha(x)=\limsup j^\alpha\|Q_jx\|$,
$0\le\alpha<1$, sublevel and exact-limit sets have dimension $1-\alpha$.
At each $k\ge1$ an explicit gauge $h_k$, $h_k(1/Q_n)=(2k+1)^{-n}$,
is exact: $\{\Lambda=k/4\}$ has infinite but $\sigma$-finite
$\mathcal{H}^{h_k}$-measure in every open arc on $\mathbf{R}/\mathbf{Z}$,
while $\{\Lambda<k/4\}$ is null; gauges are classified by
$\liminf_n(2k+1)^n g(1/Q_n)$.
Each positive level splits three ways: limsup level (base $2k+1$),
unsigned exact limits (universal binary base $2$, every $k$), and signed
exact limits (each a single countable $L$-coset).
The classification, gap, spectrum, dimension and gauge statements compiled
here are machine-checked in Lean~4 on the base axioms only; this split draft
collects the core (2A) track. It does not address the irrationality of
$e+\pi$, which remains open.
\end{abstract}
