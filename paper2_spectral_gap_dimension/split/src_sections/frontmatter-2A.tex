\begin{abstract}
For the subsequence of convergent denominators of $e$ preceding the partial
quotients $2,4,6,\dots$ we determine the complete Diophantine approximation
spectrum and its fine metric structure. With
$\Lambda(x)=\limsup_{j\to\infty} j\|Q_jx\|$ the spectrum is exactly
$\frac14\mathbf{Z}_{\ge0}\cup\{\infty\}$: every interval $(k/4,(k+1)/4)$ is
an unconditional empty gap, and $\Lambda=0$ precisely on the countable group
$L=\mathbf{Z}+\mathbf{Z}\frac{e+1}{2}$. Every finite level set has Hausdorff
dimension $0$, while at subcritical weights the dimension is exactly
$1-\alpha$. At each positive level $k/4$ we exhibit an exact Hausdorff gauge
$h_k(1/Q_n)=(2k+1)^{-n}$: the level has infinite $\sigma$-finite
$\mathcal{H}^{h_k}$-measure in every nonempty open arc of $\R/\Z$, and the
strict sublevel is null; arbitrary gauges are classified by their lower limit
along the recurrence scales. Each positive level further splits three ways:
the limsup level, unsigned exact limits with a universal binary alphabet
independent of $k$, and signed exact limits collapsing to single countable
$L$-cosets. The proofs are machine-checked in Lean~4 on the base axioms only.
This work does not address the irrationality of $e+\pi$, which remains open.
\end{abstract}
