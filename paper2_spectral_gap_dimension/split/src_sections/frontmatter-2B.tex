\begin{abstract}
For polynomial-growth recurrences $Q_{j+1}=(cj^r+d)Q_j+Q_{j-1}$ and,
more generally, arbitrary growing-quotient recurrences
$Q_{j+1}=A_jQ_j+Q_{j-1}$ with $A_j\to\infty$, we establish the full
weighted spectrum, exact critical gauges with $1/r$-scaled logarithmic
order, positive-level three-way splitting, and subcritical dimension
$1-\alpha/r$ ($0\le\alpha<r$), with collapse beyond.
The zero level is an intrinsic rank-two lattice; the $e$-convergent theory
is the special case. Three concrete growing regimes illustrate the
$\gamma_{\sup}$ phase diagram. Statements compiled here are machine-checked
in Lean~4 on the base axioms only; this split draft collects the general
theory (2B) track. It does not address the irrationality of $e+\pi$,
which remains open.
\end{abstract}
