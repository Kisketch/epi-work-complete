\begin{abstract}
For polynomial-growth recurrences $Q_{j+1}=(cj^r+d)Q_j+Q_{j-1}$ and, more
generally, arbitrary growing-quotient recurrences $Q_{j+1}=A_jQ_j+Q_{j-1}$
with $A_j\to\infty$, we establish the complete weighted spectrum
$\frac1c\mathbf{Z}_{\ge0}\cup\{\infty\}$, exact critical gauges with
$1/r$-scaled logarithmic order, the positive-level three-way splitting
relative to the intrinsic rank-two lattice, and the subcritical dimension
$1-\alpha/r$, with collapse to the lattice beyond the critical weight.
Three concrete growing regimes illustrate the $\gamma_{\sup}$ phase diagram,
including a secondary supercritical transition. The $e$-convergent theory is
the special case $A_j=4j+2$. The proofs are machine-checked in Lean~4 on the
base axioms only. This work does not address the irrationality of $e+\pi$,
which remains open.
\end{abstract}
