\begin{abstract}
Along the same denominators, Hermite window-count constraints have exact
dimensions $1$ (prescribed parity) and $1/2$ (bounded increments);
short windows occur with an explicit positive frequency almost everywhere,
while divergent window counts force full dimension $1$ and zero measure.
Eventual periodicity of continued-fraction digits modulo $4$ has the
dimension of the fixed alphabet $1\bmod4$, strictly between $357/512$
and $359/512$; for general moduli a Lipschitz sandwich, transfer-operator
criterion and exact integer certificates give
$\dim_H F_{m,m}<\dim_H F_{m,1}$ for all $m\ge2$ with reciprocal-Fibonacci
asymptotics. Finite-state tail constraints replace the base by the
maximal Perron root over active marked components. This split draft
collects the windows/residues/finite-state (2C) track. It does not
address the irrationality of $e+\pi$, which remains open.
\end{abstract}
