\begin{abstract}
Along the selected convergent denominators of $e$, Hermite window-count
constraints have exact dimensions $1$ for prescribed parity and $1/2$ for
bounded increments; short windows occur with an explicit positive frequency
almost everywhere, while divergent window counts force full Hausdorff
dimension $1$ and Lebesgue-nullity. Eventual periodicity of
continued-fraction digits modulo $4$ has the dimension of the fixed alphabet
$1\bmod4$, strictly between $357/512$ and $179/256$; for every modulus
$m\ge2$ a Lipschitz sandwich, an unconditional transfer-operator dimension
criterion and exact integer certificates give the strict separation
$\dim_H F_{m,m}<\dim_H F_{m,1}$ together with reciprocal-Fibonacci-type
large-$m$ asymptotics; for general finite multi-residue alphabets $F_{m,S,B}$,
the dimension undergoes a phase transition at $1/2$ with limit
$\max(1/2,s_0(S))$, satisfies an explicit three-term Euler--Mascheroni
expansion to $O((\log m)^2/m^3)$ in the subcritical regime, and converges at
the power-law rate $\Theta(m^{-2s_0(S)})$ in the supercritical regime
$s_0(S)>1/2$. Finite-state constraints on defect tails replace the
critical base by the maximal Perron root over active marked components. The
proofs---including the closed intervals of Table~\ref{tab:residues-general} and
the multi-residue asymptotic theory---are machine-checked in Lean~4 on the base
axioms only, while open-interval strictness in Table~\ref{tab:residues-general}
combines exact Python integer certificates with the cited pressure formalism.
This work does not address the irrationality of
$e+\pi$, which remains open.
\end{abstract}
