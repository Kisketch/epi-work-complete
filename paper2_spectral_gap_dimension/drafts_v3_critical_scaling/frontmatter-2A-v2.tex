\title{On the complete spectrum discretization and exact Hausdorff gauges
for the convergent denominators of $e$}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Let $(Q_j)_{j\ge1}$ be the selected convergent denominators of $\ee$ preceding
the partial quotients $2j$, with $Q_{j+1}=(4j+2)Q_j+Q_{j-1}$, and let
$\Lam(x)=\limsup_{j\to\infty}j\|Q_jx\|$. We prove that $\{\Lam=0\}$ is exactly
the countable group $L=\Z+\Z\frac{\ee+1}{2}$ and that the spectrum is
completely discrete, $\Lam(\R)=\frac14\Z_{\ge0}\cup\{\infty\}$: every open
interval $(k/4,(k+1)/4)$ is an unconditional empty spectral gap with sublevel
sets constant across each gap. Every finite level set has Hausdorff dimension
$0$, while $\Lam=\infty$ Lebesgue-almost everywhere (dimension $1$), and the
subcritical dimension at weight $\alpha$ is exactly $1-\alpha$. At each
positive level $k/4$ the gauge $h_k(1/Q_n)=(2k+1)^{-n}$ is exact: the level
has infinite, $\sigma$-finite $\mathcal H^{h_k}$-measure in every nonempty
open arc of $\R/\Z$, the strict sublevel is null, arbitrary gauges are
classified by their lower limit along the recurrence scales, and each positive
level splits three ways (limsup level, unsigned exact-limit set with universal
binary alphabet, signed exact-limit sets collapsing to countable $L$-cosets).
All statements of this paper are machine-checked in Lean~4 using only the base
axioms \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}; no
input of this paper lies outside the Lean kernel (audit subset below).
This work does not resolve the open problem of the irrationality of $e+\pi$.
\end{abstract}
